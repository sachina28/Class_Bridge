ClassBridge

The Student Life Companion

Academics · Mind · Body

One space to learn, feel supported and grow.

ClassBridge brings study planning, subject notes, class coordination and everyday wellbeing into one student-centered dashboard. This prototype stores entries only in this browser on this device.

Prototype privacy note: This demo is not a secure production system. Data is saved in local browser storage and may be accessible to anyone using this device/browser. Do not enter sensitive health information, passwords or private details. A real deployment needs authentication, encrypted server storage, access controls, backups, security testing and a privacy policy.
